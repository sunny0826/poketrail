# Encounter rules and queues

[中文](usage-encounters.md) · [English](usage-encounters.en.md)

[Back to the usage guide](usage.en.md#exploration-and-capture).

Forms you have captured, received as a gift, or obtained through evolution are all marked as previously obtained and leave the new-encounter pool together with all of their ancestors. Evolution and restarting do not restore eligibility. You can encounter a species again after a defeat, retreat, failed capture, or abandonment. Receiving the starter leaves 72 Kanto candidates. When a region has no candidates left, "Stats and records" shows "No new encounters · Try another". No exploration steps are consumed while you stay there. When you switch to a region that still has candidates, an encounter starts at once if your saved balance is enough. When no region has candidates, it shows "No new regular Pokémon", and step counting and training continue.

You can leave an encounter for later and keep walking to queue more. Encounters are processed in order, with room for 32 including the current encounter. A full queue or lack of eligible species leaves your exploration steps available for later. The same species cannot appear twice in the queue. Home and "Stats and records" show "Encounters 3", while the main menu shows "Resume encounter 3". Only encounters that have not started battle are counted; starting battle reduces the count by one. Confirm the completed result to move to the next encounter. No count is shown when there are none, and the menu reads "No encounter yet". The throwing entry remains "Resume catch".

Switch regions with "Choose region" in the menu. The regions are listed in generation order across two pages, and the highlighted region previews its scene. Changing region, switching partners or evolving affects newly generated encounters; saved encounters retain their original region and partner snapshot. Training steps still belong to the currently carried partner. A defeat or retreat does not cost you your partner. Poké Balls are unlimited. The ball icon on Home and "Stats and records" shows the number of distinct species captured. Each species counts once; gifts and evolution do not add to the count, and previously captured forms remain counted after evolution. On the throwing page, two balls under the title show the attempts left in this encounter; a used attempt becomes an empty ring. There is no static ball badge in the upper right.
